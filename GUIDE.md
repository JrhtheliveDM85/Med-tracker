# Med Tracker: install and use

UnityForge Labs. One page for personal use.

This is not medical advice, and it does not replace your doctor or your own judgement.

Your dose log and settings stay in this browser, on your device. There is no account, and UnityForge does not receive your dose history. You can export a backup file you keep. Clearing browser data, or using a different browser or device, will start you with an empty log unless you import that backup. The web preview does load fonts and scripts from outside services for look and feel. The app does not send your dose log to those services.

## Open or install

Open the web address you were given, in Chrome or Safari. You can also host the files from this pack yourself and open that address.

To keep it on your phone:

- Android, Chrome: open the page, then tap Install, or the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
- iPhone, Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then open Med Tracker from that icon.

Inside another app, such as LinkedIn, use the menu and choose Open in browser. Phone reminders cannot turn on inside those apps. On iPhone, reminders also stay off until you open Med Tracker from the Home Screen icon and tap Allow. If reminders cannot turn on, the app says so in plain words. You can still log a dose, and you can still use the on-screen banner and read-aloud.

The first time, the setup screen asks for a medicine name, how many pills you have, and your usual time. You can set a PIN or leave it blank. Tick the notice, then save. If the screen is taller than your phone, scroll it. Nothing you need is trapped off the bottom.

## Log a dose

Tap the big button once when you take your dose. The log stores the time on this phone. The pills-left count goes down by one. Read-aloud and the words under the button tell you what was saved. The picture of the gremlin is only a companion to those words.

## What the 12-hour lock means

The lock stops a second tap from quietly logging another dose.

You can set the lock to 12, 16, 18, or 20 hours in Settings. Twelve hours is the shortest. A new setup starts at 18 hours, the recommended lock. The button also stays locked for the rest of that day. If you are often up past midnight, the day can start at 4:00 AM instead of midnight.

While the lock is on, the button says it is locked and shows the time you logged. If you tap it anyway, the app stops and asks. Tap cancel if you already took it. Log a second dose only if you mean to.

The lock is a reminder on this phone. It cannot stop a pill you take by hand.

## Export and import a backup

Open Settings. Tap Export JSON Backup and keep that file somewhere safe, such as a folder you control. It is your log, saved as JSON.

On a new phone or after the browser data is cleared, open Settings, tap Restore from JSON, and choose that file. Your medicine name, pill count, log, and settings come back. A PIN stays on the phone where you set it. Set it again in Settings if you want one.

## Voice and themes

Tap Read to hear today's status. Tap again to stop. Each section has a speaker button that reads that part aloud. In Settings you can pick a voice, a speed, a font, and less motion.

Themes are Warm Oatmeal, Deep Slate, Soft Sage, and Twilight Violet. The chosen theme says Selected. Status is also written in words, not only by a colour or the gremlin.

If your phone asks for less motion, animations and confetti stay off. You can turn that on yourself in Settings too.

Buttons and fields are large enough to tap. If you use a keyboard, the focused control shows an outline.
